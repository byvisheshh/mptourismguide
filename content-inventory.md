### B. UNESCO World Heritage & Historic Forts
3. **Khajuraho Temples (`/destinations/khajuraho-temples`)**
   - *Core Subject*: Chandela Dynasty 10th-11th century Nagara-style sandstone architectural masterpieces, Western & Eastern temple groups, Kandariya Mahadeva temple, erotic and secular lifestyle friezes.
4. **Sanchi Stupa (`/destinations/sanchi-stupa`)**
   - *Core Subject*: Emperor Ashoka’s 3rd century BCE Great Stupa, ornamental Torana gateways depicting Jataka tales, Buddhist monastery ruins, Ashoka Pillar.
5. **Orchha Medieval Heritage (`/destinations/orchha`)**
   - *Core Subject*: Bundela Rajput capital established by Rudra Pratap Singh in 1501, Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal with vibrant murals, Ram Raja Temple (where Rama is revered as king), 14 royal Chhatris along the Betwa River.
6. **Gwalior Fort (`/destinations/gwalior-fort`)**
   - *Core Subject*: "The Gibraltar of India" perched atop Gopachal sandstone hill, Raja Man Singh Tomar’s Man Mandir Palace with turquoise-glazed mosaic tiles, Teli Ka Mandir, rock-cut Jain Colossi.
7. **Bhimbetka Rock Shelters (`/destinations/bhimbetka`)**
   - *Core Subject*: 30,000-year-old Paleolithic and Mesolithic rock art, natural sandstone shelters discovered by Dr. V.S. Wakankar in 1957, mineral pigments of ochre and hematite.
8. **Mandu — The City of Joy (`/destinations/mandu`)**
   - *Core Subject*: Malwa Sultanate citadel atop the Vindhyas, Jahaz Mahal floating between Kapoor and Munj Talao lakes, Hindola Mahal with sloping walls, romantic pavilions of Rani Roopmati and Baz Bahadur.

### C. Nature & Hill Stations
9. **Pachmarhi Hill Station (`/destinations/pachmarhi`)**
   - *Core Subject*: 1,067m elevation in the Satpura range, Bee Falls, Duchess Falls, Dhoopgarh (highest peak in MP at 1,352m), Pandav Caves, cool sal and pine canopies.
10. **Bhopal & The City of Lakes (`/destinations/bhopal`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Bhojtal (Upper Lake) created by Raja Bhoj, Van Vihar National Park, Taj-ul-Masajid, Madhya Pradesh Tribal Museum, Bharat Bhavan.
11. **Indore City & Commercial Capital (`/destinations/indore`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Holkar dynasty legacy, 7-story Rajwada wooden-stone palace, Lal Bagh Palace European opulence, Kanch Mandir glass temple.
12. **Chitrakoot Ramayana Pilgrimage (`/destinations/chitrakoot`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Sacred exile forest where Lord Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana spent 11.5 years, Ram Ghat on Mandakini River, Kamadgiri circumambulation, Gupt Godavari caves.

### D. Tiger Reserves & Wildlife Sanctuaries
13. **Bandhavgarh National Park (`/wildlife/bandhavgarh-national-park`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Highest documented density of Royal Bengal Tigers in India, historic 2,000-year-old Bandhavgarh Fort, Shesh Shaiya reclining Vishnu statue, Tala, Magdhi, and Khitauli safari zones.
14. **Kanha Tiger Reserve (`/wildlife/kanha-national-park`)**
    - *Core Subject*: 1,945 sq km pristine Sal forest and grassy maidans, home of the endangered Hardground Barasingha (state animal of MP), inspiration for Rudyard Kipling’s *The Jungle Book*.
15. **Pench Tiger Reserve (`/wildlife/pench-national-park`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Mowgli’s stomping ground traversing the Seoni and Chhindwara districts, teak and bamboo woods, Turia and Karmajhiri safari entries.
16. **Satpura Tiger Reserve (`/wildlife/satpura-national-park`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Unique walking safaris permitted inside core zones, boat safaris on the Denwa River, leopard and sloth bear habitat in rugged gorges.

### E. Malwa Culinary Traditions & Street Food
17. **Indore Poha & Jalebi (`/food-and-cuisine/indore-poha-jalebi`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Steam-cooked flattened rice infused with turmeric, fennel, and diced onions, crowned with crispy Ratlami Sev, pomegranate pearls, and proprietary Jeeravan spice powder, accompanied by crisp saffron jalebis.
18. **Dal Bafla Tradition (`/food-and-cuisine/dal-bafla`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Heritage Malwa delicacy: whole-wheat dough balls spiced with ajwain, boiled in bubbling water, baked over cow-dung cakes or charcoal till cracked, submerged in hot pure desi ghee, served with panchmel tuvar dal, tart baigan bharta, and fiery garlic chutney.
19. **Sarafa Bazaar Midnight Food Market (`/food-and-cuisine/sarafa-bazaar-indore`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Historic silver and gold jewelry bullion market by day, transforming into a bustling street gastronomy carnival from 8:30 PM to 2:00 AM. Famous for Bhutte ka Kees, spicy deep-fried Garadu roots with chaat masala, and fire paan.
20. **Bhopali Biryani, Kebabs & Tea (`/food-and-cuisine/bhopali-biryani-and-kebabs`)**
    - *Core Subject*: Begums of Bhopal royal culinary legacy: aromatic dum-cooked goat mutton biryani with mild spices, silky melt-in-the-mouth Shami kebabs, minced Filfora, and hot pink Sulemani namkeen tea.
