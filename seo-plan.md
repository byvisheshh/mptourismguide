## 1. Executive SEO Strategy & Search Intent Model

This portal is structured according to topical authority clusters around the central entity: **Madhya Pradesh Tourism** (fondly acclaimed as the *"Heart of Incredible India"*). Search behavior is bifurcated into four high-intent visitor segments:
1. **Pilgrims & Spiritual Travelers**: Seeking darshan timings, rituals (e.g., Bhasma Aarti, Narmada Parikrama), distance between Jyotirlingas (Ujjain & Omkareshwar), and travel logistics.
2. **Heritage & Architecture Enthusiasts**: Researching UNESCO World Heritage monuments (Khajuraho, Sanchi, Bhimbetka), Chandela dynasties, Bundelkhand forts (Orchha, Gwalior), and Parmara palaces (Mandu).
3. **Wildlife & Eco-Tourists**: Looking for tiger density, core vs. buffer safari zones, booking permits, best season for wildlife viewing in Bandhavgarh, Kanha, Pench, and Satpura.
4. **Food & Culture Tourists**: Craving authentic Malwa culinary experiences, Indore street food (Sarafa Bazaar & Chappan Dukan), Dal Bafla, Bhopali Nawabi biryani, and regional handloom crafts (Chanderi & Maheshwari).

## 3. Structured Data (Schema.org) Mapping
- **BreadcrumbList**: Rendered on all child routes (`/destinations/*`, `/wildlife/*`, `/food-and-cuisine/*`, `/travel-guides/*`).
- **TouristAttraction**: Embedded on all destination and wildlife pages (Omkareshwar, Mahakaleshwar, Khajuraho, Sanchi, Gwalior Fort, Orchha, Bandhavgarh, Kanha, etc.).
- **Recipe / FoodEstablishment**: Embedded on culinary guide pages (`/food-and-cuisine/indore-poha-jalebi`, `/food-and-cuisine/dal-bafla`, etc.).
- **TouristTrip / Guide**: Embedded on itinerary routes (`/travel-guides/*`).
- **WebSite / Organization**: Embedded on the root homepage (`/`).

## 4. Crawlability & Indexing Directives
- **Self-referencing Canonical URLs**: Every route dynamic canonical URL is set via `document.head` manipulation to prevent duplicate parameters.
- **Sitemap XML**: Valid XML hosted at `/sitemap.xml` with `<loc>`, `<lastmod>`, `<changefreq>`, and `<priority>`.
- **Robots.txt**: Valid robots file hosted at `/robots.txt` granting full user-agent access and referencing `/sitemap.xml`.
