## 1. Asset Verification Standards
In strict accordance with project rules, every photographic asset has been verified against the exact physical monument, temple, wildlife sanctuary, or culinary preparation it represents. No generic cityscapes or unrelated temples have been substituted for named historical or sacred landmarks.
